# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Vinext / React / TypeScript / Tailwind v4 starter, delegated by the user to Codex for implementation and Sites deployment.

## Users

Primary users are visually discerning Chinese-speaking visitors who want to enter a complete science-fiction work, not merely view a decorative landing page. They should be able to understand the world quickly, make one meaningful choice, and leave with a personal reflection.

## Product Purpose

Create an interactive science-fiction narrative that combines high-end cinematic imagery with a legible web experience. Success means the visitor feels the pressure of a future public institution, understands the ethical conflict around removing painful memories, completes a consequential interaction, and receives a thoughtful ending that reflects their choice.

## Positioning

The work treats the future as a believable public system rather than neon spectacle. Its central mechanism asks the visitor to calibrate what a city should remember, then turns that choice into a cinematic and philosophical consequence.

## Operating Context

The experience is a single responsive web journey in Simplified Chinese. It opens as a film-like first viewport, moves through a compact evidence archive, culminates in a memory-calibration interaction, and closes with a shareable personal verdict. It must work with pointer, keyboard, and touch input.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-route interactive narrative.
- Original generated cinematic imagery is part of the finished work.
- No login, external data, persistence, invented commercial claims, or factual metrics.
- The visitor's choice may be stored only in device-local state during the session.
- Motion must degrade safely under reduced-motion preferences.
- The work must remain coherent on desktop and mobile.
- Visible copy and controls are in Simplified Chinese.

## Brand Commitments

- Working title: 《余光协议》.
- Science fiction must come from institutions, infrastructure, and human choices, not default cyberpunk decoration.
- The result must be content-rich, aesthetically exacting, and meaningful in visual and spiritual terms.
- `taste-skill` and `cinema-dna-21x9x3` are binding creative methods.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the creative brief and required skills. No external testimonials, customers, research data, proprietary assets, or factual performance claims were supplied and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The moral choice is the product, not a decorative theme.
2. Show the future through ordinary people facing an abnormal institution.
3. Let one precise visual decision carry more weight than many effects.
4. Every interaction must deepen the story or clarify consequence.
5. The ending should respect ambiguity and invite reflection rather than reward a "correct" answer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, responsive layout, reduced-motion behavior, and descriptive alternative text are required.

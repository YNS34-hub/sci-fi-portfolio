## Representative

**TEAR/LINE** is the representative work. It has the clearest one-sentence idea, the strongest first frame, the highest photographic art direction and an interaction that is simultaneously navigation, mask, chapter transition and metaphor.

The ultimate pass did not add generic polish. It deepened that single idea: drag velocity now becomes tear tension, widening the code-native fiber and slipping the under-image while the pointer is held. The end-to-end suite verifies the chapter wheel, actual pointer capture, tear-position delta and live fiber-width response.

## Originality Boundary

The nine references informed pacing, restraint, material contrast and interaction ambition. No reference’s page hierarchy, visual identity, copy, logo, layout sequence or signature interaction was reproduced. Composition, crop, masks, type, interaction and project-native procedural textures are original; Site 03 alone uses four licensed, source-recorded Unsplash photographs. Every required label remains semantic HTML.

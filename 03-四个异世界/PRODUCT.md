# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, GSAP and Playwright in one deployable project with five independent routes. This keeps the four experiences separately addressable while making the gallery easy to run and deploy.

## Users

The primary audience is creative directors, senior designers, creative developers, studio recruiters and design-award reviewers viewing the work on high-performance desktop screens or through desktop recordings.

## Product Purpose

Create four complete original creative-web experiences, plus a gallery entry point, that demonstrate distinct visual authorship, interaction design, motion direction and 3D/WebGL craft. Success means each experience is memorable in its first viewport, supports a coherent full-page journey and survives real multi-resolution review.

## Positioning

This is not one flexible template with four themes. Each route behaves like a work from a different specialist studio, with a separate content model, spatial logic, motion rhythm, input language and final visual memory.

## Operating Context

The work is evaluated primarily at 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 3840x2160 on desktop. Visitors use a mouse and wheel. Mobile access must remain readable and stable, with graceful reduction of expensive graphics. The project is expected to support portfolio review, desktop screen recording and public deployment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Research nine named creative references and document original transferable principles before production code.
- Deliver four independent routes and one gallery route.
- Include high-resolution visual assets, real motion systems, mouse feedback and meaningful scroll choreography.
- Site 01 centers cinematic future luxury and a precision 3D object.
- Site 02 centers a complete physics-like digital-toy interaction.
- Site 03 centers kinetic editorial typography, image choreography and horizontal chapters.
- Site 04 centers a three-stage spatial journey and one directly controllable 3D artifact.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences and degrade complex WebGL on constrained/mobile clients.
- Do not copy reference code, brand language, page structures, media, models or distinctive animations.
- Do not invent commercial proof, customers, prices or performance claims.
- Maintain the required planning, research, build, QA, comparison and final-report documents throughout the project.

## Brand Commitments

The four final brands are original and must remain visibly unrelated to the nine references and to one another. The work must avoid generic AI-template conventions, blue-purple SaaS gradients, repeated card grids, default Three.js spheres and filler copy.

## Product Principles

1. Research principles, then originate the expression.
2. Let interaction and motion advance meaning rather than decorate empty structure.
3. Make the first viewport a demonstrable thesis.
4. Author four different systems, not four themed skins.
5. Treat screenshots, console evidence and real user-flow checks as design inputs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary craft targets desktop, but all routes must retain readable copy, keyboard focus, usable navigation and stable layout on smaller screens. Reduced-motion users receive an intentional static or low-motion composition rather than a broken experience.

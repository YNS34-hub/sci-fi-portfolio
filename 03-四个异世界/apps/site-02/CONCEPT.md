# Site 02 Concept — GRAMMAR WEATHER

## Product Truth

An experimental digital toy lets visitors type a word and observe it as matter. The work must be playable, reversible and readable, not a passive particle demo.

## Seven Grounded Directions

1. **Ink Basin Syntax** — words alter simulated viscosity and vorticity.
2. **Pressure-Plotter Choir** — glyphs draw and erase a shared waveform.
3. **Magnetic Punctuation Field** — punctuation defines attractors and repulsors.
4. **Alphabet Storm** — letters condense into words, split into weather and reshape a clean field according to a small grammar.
5. **Semantic Tide Table** — word length and vowels determine cyclic waves.
6. **Breathing Typespecimen** — variable axes respond to pointer pressure and proximity.
7. **Phoneme Foundry** — letter groups collide and alloy into new glyph matter.

## Selected Direction

Candidate 4, selected by concept seed `708257fe`. It has the clearest one-line invitation and can sustain both spectacle and meaningful state. The site uses a deterministic local rule set, not AI-generated semantic claims.

## First-Surface Structure

1. Paper-white field with one example word already forming.
2. Small title and status sentence at top left.
3. Accessible text input and submit control at bottom left.
4. Three force verbs, “Gather / Shear / Rain,” aligned to the input.
5. A compact upper-right event log narrates the current transformation.
6. Pointer movement bends the local vector field without obscuring the word.

## Required Prototype Capability

A canvas or WebGL particle system must create glyph-sampled points, deterministic forces, pointer wind, readable-to-dispersed state transitions and a true reset. Keyboard and reduced-motion alternatives must expose the same conceptual states.

## Three Iterations

- **R1 — Complete toy loop:** input, formation, three forces, reset and accessible state narration.
- **R2 — Physical character:** tuned inertia, local pointer wind, glyph sampling and responsive controls.
- **R3 — Visual authority:** density, trace quality, large-screen composition, performance and reduced motion.

## Originality Guardrails

Do not imitate Thingy’s horoscope premise, illustrations or compatibility mechanism. Do not default to a neon generative-art aesthetic. The white field, legible word states and reversible grammar are the identity.

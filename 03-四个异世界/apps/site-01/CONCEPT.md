# Site 01 Concept — VANTA/FORM

## Product Truth

A fictional future-luxury atelier presents one precision object through assembly, material evidence and cinematic restraint. The page must make craft legible without inventing commercial claims.

## Seven Grounded Directions

1. **Smoked Quartz Reliquary** — one object seen through sliding optical planes.
2. **Orbital Calibration Room** — camera travel follows measurement rings around a fixed instrument.
3. **Magnetic Seam Altar** — seams attract and repel as the user changes tolerances.
4. **Refractive Time Archive** — an object is read at three optical distances.
5. **Zero-Gravity Atelier** — components wait at authored coordinates, then settle.
6. **Ceremonial Assembly Ledger** — six parts descend along drawn axes, each step pairing a tactile 3D change with one material fact.
7. **Black-Mirror Auction** — controlled light reveals provenance details around a rotating silhouette.

## Selected Direction

Candidate 6, selected by concept seed `245ee642`. It identifies as luxury through finish, pace and proportion while making the first interaction immediately understandable. The literal toy-book colors are rejected; the assembly grammar is translated into linen drafting stock, obsidian space and burnished metal.

## First-Surface Structure

1. Quiet atelier mark and exhibition exit at the top edge.
2. Deep black 3D bay occupying roughly 68% of the viewport.
3. Bone ledger margin with step number, part name, one material fact and an assembly progress rail.
4. The object begins visibly incomplete; one component seats within the first wheel movement.
5. A small “drag to inspect” control appears only after assembly.

## Required Prototype Capability

A real WebGL object built from at least six custom geometries must support exploded/assembled interpolation, pointer rotation, material-state lighting and a static fallback. No default primitives may remain visually recognizable as the finished product.

## Three Iterations

- **R1 — Spatial thesis:** object silhouette, split architecture, assembly progression.
- **R2 — Material credibility:** lighting, metal/quartz shaders, typography and specification rhythm.
- **R3 — Cinematic finish:** camera transitions, hover/drag states, reduced motion, large-screen calibration.

## Originality Guardrails

Do not reuse Alche’s triangular letter-object, curved grid chamber or giant wordmark. Do not reproduce Cappen’s centered black object under a giant slogan. Assembly is a functional narrative, not ornamental exploded-view imagery.

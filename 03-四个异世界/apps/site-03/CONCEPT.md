# Site 03 Concept — TEAR/LINE

## Product Truth

A fictional independent fashion editorial presents one collection as four successive public images. Reading means peeling the campaign’s surface back to discover how its image changed.

## Seven Grounded Directions

1. **Runway Contact Film** — a moving negative strip alternates full silhouettes and seam details.
2. **Folded Pattern Atlas** — garment patterns open into photographed looks.
3. **Overprinted Casting Wall** — portraits align by registration marks and misprint under motion.
4. **Décollage Timeline** — the navigation is a responsive tear exposing older campaign layers beneath.
5. **Tailor’s Light Table** — translucent layers compare sketch, pattern, fitting and final look.
6. **Drawcord Issue** — one continuous cord gathers and releases editorial spreads.
7. **Three-Distance Lookbook** — each look swaps between silhouette, styling and construction views.

## Selected Direction

Candidate 4, selected by concept seed `9a5e1575`. The torn timeline gives the editorial a unique reading verb and lets typography, photography and chronology operate in one gesture. It stays premium through art direction and controlled rhythm rather than polished-card conventions.

## First-Surface Structure

1. Full-bleed horizontal campaign panel with no boxed content.
2. Masthead and issue metadata pinned to opposing corners.
3. One enormous vertical collection word crossing the edge.
4. A fibrous tear opens from the right to reveal the prior campaign image below.
5. Wheel and pointer motion both advance the tear; a visible chapter rail provides direct access.

## Required Prototype Capability

At least four original editorial images must be arranged in a horizontal sequence. SVG/CSS masks must create a responsive, controllable tear edge with parallax between layers, while semantic chapter copy remains selectable and keyboard reachable.

## Three Iterations

- **R1 — Editorial sequence:** horizontal architecture, full-bleed photography and stable chapter navigation.
- **R2 — Tear system:** pointer pull, scroll choreography, layer persistence and typographic collisions.
- **R3 — Print finish:** fiber, halftone, registration behavior, responsive crops and large-display rhythm.

## Originality Guardrails

Do not copy Twice’s restrained cinematic chrome or imagery. Do not borrow Working Stiff’s characters, retro comedy palette or exact horizontal scenes. The tear is structural and historically layered, not a generic ripped-paper decoration.

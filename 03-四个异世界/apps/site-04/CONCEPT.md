# Site 04 Concept — THE PALE BELOW

## Product Truth

An original unknown-world expedition asks the visitor to descend through three spatial chambers and directly inspect one impossible fossil instrument. It is an encounter, not a game level or lore dump.

## Seven Grounded Directions

1. **Pressure Cathedral** — architecture appears only where pointer pressure illuminates it.
2. **Mesophotic Wall** — the journey follows an exact vertical depth axis and changing visibility.
3. **Pollen Moon Transit** — drifting biological clouds reveal three distant habitats.
4. **Folded Gravity Map** — a map becomes the terrain as the visitor crosses its creases.
5. **Echo Salt Archive** — soundless sonar rings expose a buried memory field.
6. **Blind Observatory** — Threshold, Vein and Choir are revealed by a sparse probe beam; a translucent fossil instrument wakes and records the visitor’s drag.
7. **Silt Time Engine** — sediment layers move around a stationary observer and uncover an artifact.

## Selected Direction

Candidate 6, selected by concept seed `7fead5c2`. It best satisfies the three-stage spatial journey and direct artifact control without relying on familiar spacecraft, planets or fantasy ruins. Its scientific framing makes the unknown more credible.

## First-Surface Structure

1. Almost-black Threshold chamber with a narrow coordinate title.
2. A probe beam follows the pointer and reveals local spores and mineral edges.
3. The depth rail at the left shows three named chambers and current descent.
4. The artifact is initially only a silhouette. Its emissive seams appear at the Vein.
5. At the Choir, the artifact detaches from the environment and becomes draggable.

## Required Prototype Capability

A WebGL scene must provide three authored camera/environment states, depth-linked lighting and fog, a non-default custom artifact, direct rotation with inertial settling, pointer illumination and static/capability fallbacks. The same DOM must narrate visited depth and object state.

## Three Iterations

- **R1 — Journey legibility:** three chambers, camera anchors, route rail and artifact interaction.
- **R2 — World density:** custom geometry, spores, probe response, light/fog transitions.
- **R3 — Immersive finish:** shader texture, inertial drag, dramatic final compression, performance and accessibility.

## Originality Guardrails

Do not reproduce Joseph’s teal underwater gallery, Alche’s central logo-object or common planet/portal imagery. No generic starfield, glowing sphere, sci-fi HUD frame or game inventory.

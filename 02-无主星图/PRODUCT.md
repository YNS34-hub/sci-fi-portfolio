# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: preserve the initialized Vinext, React, TypeScript and Sites stack because the user delegated implementation and wants a finished deployable work.

## Users

Primary users are Chinese-speaking visitors with high visual expectations who are willing to spend several minutes exploring a mature science-fiction universe. They are not looking for a generic space gallery; they should understand the worlds, confront the central value question and complete a personal non-contact charter.

## Product Purpose

Create a complete interactive science-fiction series about whether intelligent life has the right to encounter, name and exploit every world it can reach. Success means the visitor experiences several materially distinct planets, understands that observation can itself become possession, and leaves with a personal rule for how civilization should meet the unknown.

## Positioning

The work presents a universe atlas whose most important function is not navigation but restraint. Instead of rewarding conquest, discovery or resource extraction, its mechanism asks the visitor to decide what must remain unclaimed.

## Operating Context

The experience is one responsive web journey in Simplified Chinese. It opens with an astronomical atlas, enters three planetary chapters through nine original 21:9 live-action cinematic records, lets the visitor compare scientific value against ecological autonomy, and closes with a locally generated non-contact charter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-route interactive narrative and image-rich universe atlas.
- Nine original high-resolution 21:9 cinematic images arranged as three triptych series.
- Three planetary dossiers with distinct environments, life systems and human conflicts.
- Interactive star-map navigation, chapter selection and a final charter builder.
- Visitor choices exist only in local page state and are never uploaded.
- No login, external data, commercial claims, fabricated research or server persistence.
- Responsive, keyboard and touch operable, with a reduced-motion fallback.
- All visible controls, messages and explanatory copy are in Simplified Chinese.
- The work must be clearly different from the previous project 《余光协议》 in premise, imagery, palette, spatial grammar and interaction.

## Brand Commitments

- Working title: 《无主星图》.
- English title: ATLAS OF THE UNCLAIMED.
- `pbakaus/impeccable` is the primary design method.
- `cinema-dna-21x9x3` is binding for all cinematic records.
- Science fiction comes from believable expeditions, ecological systems, political restraint and material spacecraft, not neon cyberpunk or glossy CGI.
- The result must be content-rich, image-rich, aesthetically exacting and valuable in both visual and spiritual terms.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the creative brief and required skills. All planets, expeditions, institutions and witness records in the work are fictional and must be labeled as part of the artwork. No real scientific discovery, customer, testimonial, benchmark or institutional endorsement was supplied and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The universe is not empty merely because humans cannot read its life.
2. Discovery is a relationship with consequences, not a neutral act.
3. Every image must reveal a decision in progress, not merely display a beautiful planet.
4. Planetary difference must be carried by ecology, material, light and human behavior.
5. The ending should produce a durable ethic of restraint without preaching a single correct answer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, descriptive alternative text, touch-sized interactions, responsive composition and reduced-motion behavior are required.
